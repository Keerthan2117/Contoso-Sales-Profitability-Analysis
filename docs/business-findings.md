Business Findings

Overview

I used my Power BI dashboard to analyse sales performance, product profitability, customer contribution, and geographic trends in the Contoso dataset.

1. Overall Sales Performance

Across the reporting period, the dashboard shows:

- Total Revenue: 218.81M
- Total Cost: 96.48M
- Total Profit: 122.33M
- Profit Margin: 55.91%
- Total Quantity Sold: 703,621

These KPIs provide an overview of the business's sales and profitability. Profit is calculated by subtracting product cost from revenue; it does not include other operating expenses.

2. Product Profitability

The top 10 products generated approximately 10.87M in profit, contributing around 8.89% of total profit over the full reporting period.

This analysis helps identify the products that contribute most to profitability and makes it easier to compare product-level performance.

3. Customer Performance

The top 10% of customers contributed approximately 57.45% of total revenue, representing around 125.71M in revenue.

This finding highlights the importance of understanding customer contribution and monitoring how much the business depends on its highest-revenue customers.

4. Sales Trends Over Time

I analysed monthly revenue trends and compared performance across different periods using month-over-month and year-over-year measures.

These comparisons help identify changes in sales performance and understand how revenue develops over time. The dashboard also supports year-to-date analysis.

5. Geographic and Store Performance

I compared revenue across countries and identified the top 10 stores by revenue.

These views help compare geographic performance, identify high-performing stores, and explore differences in sales across locations.

Conclusion

This project helped me apply data cleaning, data modelling, DAX calculations, and interactive visualisation to a business dataset. The dashboard brings together sales, profitability, customer, and geographic analysis to support data-driven decision-making.

Notes and Limitations

- Figures are based on the report's current calculations and filters; displayed values may be rounded.
- The currency denomination should be confirmed before assigning a currency symbol.
- Profit represents revenue minus product cost, not net profit after operating expenses.
- The findings describe observed patterns in the dataset and do not establish the causes of those patterns.
