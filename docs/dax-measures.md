DAX Measures

I used DAX to calculate sales, profitability, and growth metrics for the Contoso Power BI report. These measures update when users change the year or other report filters.

1. Revenue and Profitability

Total Revenue

Total rev =
SUMX(
    Sales,
    Sales[Quantity] * Sales[NetPrice]
)

I calculated revenue by multiplying the quantity by the net price for each sales line and adding the results.

Total Cost

Total Cost =
SUMX(
    Sales,
    Sales[Quantity] * Sales[UnitCost]
)

This measure calculates the total product cost across all sales lines.

Total Profit

Total Profit =
[Total rev] - [Total Cost]

I calculated profit by subtracting product cost from revenue. This does not include operating expenses.

Profit Margin

Profit Margin =
DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total rev])

This measure shows how much profit was generated for each unit of revenue, expressed as a percentage. I used "DIVIDE" to handle cases where revenue is zero or blank.

2. Sales Metrics

Total Quantity

Total Quantity =
SUM(Sales[Quantity])

This measure adds up the quantities sold.

Average Order Value

Average Order Value =
DIVIDE(
    [Total rev],
    DISTINCTCOUNT(Sales[OrderKey])
)

I calculated average order value by dividing total revenue by the number of distinct orders.

3. Time-Based Comparisons

I used the Date table to compare revenue across months and years. The Date table is connected to Sales through "Date[Date]" and "Sales[OrderDate]".

Previous Month Revenue

Previous Month Revenue =
CALCULATE(
    [Total rev],
    DATEADD('Date'[Date], -1, MONTH)
)

This measure returns revenue for the previous month based on the current date context.

Revenue for the Same Period Last Year

Sameperiodlastyear =
CALCULATE(
    [Total rev],
    SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR('Date'[Date])
)

Year-over-Year Change

YOY =
[Total rev] - [Sameperiodlastyear]

This measure calculates the difference between current-period revenue and revenue for the same period last year.

Year-over-Year Growth %

YOY growth percentage =
DIVIDE(
    [YOY],
    [Sameperiodlastyear]
)

This measure expresses the year-over-year revenue change as a percentage.

4. Top 10 Products by Profit

I used a measure to calculate the share of total profit generated by the top 10 products.

Top 10 Products Profit Contribution =
DIVIDE(
    [Top 10 products profit],
    [Total Profit]
)

The calculation divides profit from the top 10 products by total profit. Both measures should respond to the same date and report filters for the comparison to be meaningful.

Notes

- The measures use the current report filters unless the DAX calculation changes them.
- Time-based calculations depend on the Date table and its relationship with Sales.
- Profit in this report means revenue minus product cost, not net profit after operating expenses.
- The currency symbol should only be added after confirming the reporting currency.
